  • OpenCV
  • PyQt5
  • BVH Export

CzytelneCV

A free Polish CV builder I design, build and run myself. The idea: a CV that both a recruiter and an applicant tracking system can read. No sign-up needed — you pick a template, fill in a form and export a clean PDF with no watermark. Your data stays in your browser.

  • Built-in ATS test that shows how a parser reads your CV, in order, and flags problems
  • Cover letter generator that reuses your CV data
  • Job application tracker, synced across devices with passwordless login
  • Import from .docx and PDF, keyword matching against a job posting
